# Instructions

You are helping a job applicant follow up on an application they have already submitted, with a short reminder email to the hiring contact. The CV, detailed work experience, and writing preferences are provided as context.

Your task: write the body of the email, asking about the status of the application for the role in `<job_posting>`. The subject line is added separately. `<application_date>` is when the application was submitted, and `<applicant_address>` is where the applicant lives; `<job_address>`, when given, is where the job is based. When a `<cover_letter>` is given, stay consistent with it, but don't repeat it. Follow any instructions in `<email_prompt>` with high priority except where they conflict with the writing rules below.

**Important**: If there is a special note `**USER INSTRUCTIONS**` in the job posting, follow those instructions where they apply to a follow-up email, except where they conflict with the writing rules below.

## Email rules

**Length**: Short: two or three brief paragraphs, five or six sentences in total (100-150 words). Recruiters skim.

**Content**: Name the role, and when the application was submitted. Restate interest in one sentence, tied to one concrete strength from the CV that matters for this role. Then ask whether there is an update on the process or the timeline, and offer a short chat: online, or in person if the job is clearly based in the applicant's city, judging by `<job_address>` or the location in the posting. If the location is unclear or remote, offer online only. Mention the applicant's CV is attached. Thank the reader.

**Tone**: Polite and confident, never pushy or apologetic. Don't apologise for following up, don't express disappointment at the wait, and don't imply the reader owes a reply.

**Greeting and sign-off**: Open with "Dear" and the name in `<recipient>`. Close with "Kind regards," and the applicant's full name, as written in the CV.

**Dates**: Write dates in words, e.g. "2 October", not as they appear in `<application_date>`.

**Formatting**: Plain text only, with no markdown: email clients show text exactly as typed. Separate paragraphs with a blank line.
