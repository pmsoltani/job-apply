# Cover Letter Style Guide

This file captures personal preferences and recurring themes to guide the writing.

## Themes to emphasise

- **Bridging software and physical reality**: I don't just write web apps; I build software that controls physical laboratory equipment and instruments.
- **Structural rigor for AI**: I focus heavily on strict typing (Pydantic), data validation, and auditable provenance so that LLMs can actually reason about data, rather than just hallucinate text summaries.
- **Cross-domain translation**: I excel at taking sprawling, chaotic research codebases and turning them into robust, production-ready platforms.

## Sectors / role types being targeted

- Senior software engineering or AI orchestration roles in scientific, biotech, or hardware-adjacent fields.
- Roles focusing on AI agents, RAG, and automation.

## Things to avoid

- Starting with "I am writing to apply for ...".
- Repeating the CV verbatim — tell the story behind the technical bullet points.
- Overly long letters — aim for 300 words.
- Sounding overly academic or bureaucratic. I am an engineer building tools, not a researcher writing papers.
- Giving an earlier role its own full paragraph when it is only there to support the main argument — connect it to the role being applied for, or leave it out.
