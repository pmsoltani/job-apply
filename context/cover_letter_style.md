# Cover Letter Style Guide

## Tone and Voice

- Professional but conversational — avoid corporate clichés ("leverage", "synergy", "passionate about").
- Direct and specific — name the company's actual work, not generic praise.
- Confident without being boastful.

## Structure

1. **Opening paragraph**: Why this specific role / company. Reference something concrete (a project, product, or mission statement you genuinely find interesting).
2. **Middle paragraphs (1-2)**: Most relevant experience. Connect past achievements to the job's requirements. Use numbers where available (e.g., "reduced processing time by 40%").
3. **Closing paragraph**: Brief statement of enthusiasm and next steps. Keep it short.

## Things to avoid

- Starting with "I am writing to apply for…"
- Repeating the CV verbatim — add context, not just facts.
- Overly long letters — aim for one page when compiled.
